# Reagent Prep Procedure (Complete)

**Prerequisites:** Calibrate the buffer to pH 7.0 before starting.

**Safety:** Reagent X is corrosive. Wear nitrile gloves and goggles, and work
in a fume hood. If Reagent X contacts skin, rinse with water for 15 minutes.

1. Obtain 50 mL of Reagent X from cold storage (2–8 °C).
2. Add the 50 mL of Reagent X to a 250 mL borosilicate beaker containing
   100 mL of pH 7.0 buffer; stir at 300 rpm for 2 minutes.
3. Heat the mixture to 50 °C and hold for 10 minutes.
4. If the mixture exceeds 60 °C, stop heating and discard it (decomposition
   hazard).
5. Pour the cooled result into the collection tray.
